## Platform

web

## Stack

Redesign may replace the current React + Vite + Hono stack entirely. The founders prefer **Svelte (SvelteKit)**. Hosting **stays on Cloudflare** (Workers/Pages).

## Users

Decision-makers at **mid-sized companies** who are weighing up whether to hire an outside software partner. They arrive with an operational problem: disconnected systems, manual processes or data spread across tools. They want to know whether DA FØLD is credible enough to talk to.

The current lead pipeline is lukewarm and cold leads from the UnoAfrica system. Many visitors will arrive from that outreach rather than from search.

## Product Purpose

DA FØLD offers **custom software engineering and consulting**. It is a services business, not a product platform.

The website exists to **build credibility for sales**. When a lead from outreach checks out the company, the site should make DA FØLD look serious, capable and safe to contact. Success means a lead books a conversation.

## Positioning

**Open decision.** The founders have not yet established what sets DA FØLD apart and expect to learn it from the first client engagements. Until then, future work must not invent one.

The existing site copy leans on "unify your operations" and "enterprise excellence". This is current copy, not a confirmed position.

## Operating Context

- Leads come mainly from the UnoAfrica system, with cold and lukewarm outreach.
- The contact form posts to `https://dafold.com/api/send-message` (name, email, project type, details).
- References the founders like: Entelect (entelect.co.za) and iSprout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: custom software engineering and consulting.
- Team: two founders. The team is **kept anonymous** on the site for now, with no names, photos or bios. One founder holds a BSc in Software Engineering. That fact may be used without a name, if helpful.
- **Undecided:** vision and mission statements (drafts exist but are not approved), and the differentiator.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **DA FØLD**, always with the Ø.
- A brand manual exists (PDF, kept off-repo) but the founders consider it poorly executed. Treat it as a source for **color hex codes only**, not as a binding visual direction.
- The palette from the manual should live in code as design tokens. Colors currently in code are navy `#1A2F3A`, teal `#54D6C4` and off-white `#f3f5f2`. **Check these against the manual before treating them as final.**
- Logo: only raster files are in this repo (`public/logo.png`, `src/react-app/assets/logo.jpg`). An SVG may exist in the DA FØLD CRM repository, which is not yet accessible.
- A typography rule exists in the brand materials but has not yet been provided.

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no clients, case studies, testimonials, logos, metrics or partnerships yet. **Future work must not fabricate any of these.** Credibility has to come from clarity, craft and honest framing of capability, not invented proof.

## Product Principles

1. **Earn trust without proof.** With no clients yet, the site itself is the evidence. Its quality, clarity and precision must show the standard of work.
2. **Speak to the mid-sized operator.** Address concrete operational pain in plain language, not abstract enterprise buzzwords.
3. **Honest over impressive.** Claim only what is true. Leave a gap as a gap rather than filling it with invented numbers or logos.
4. **One clear next step.** Every page leads to a conversation with the founders.
